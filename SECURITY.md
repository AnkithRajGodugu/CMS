## Reporting a Vulnerability
We take the security of the CMS enterprise platform seriously. If you discover a security vulnerability, we appreciate your help in disclosing it to us in a responsible manner.

### How to Report
1. Please do NOT open a public issue or pull request for security vulnerabilities.
2. Email your findings to security@example-cms.local or the repository administrators.
3. Include as much information as possible: 
   - Type of vulnerability (e.g., XSS, SQLi, CSRF, etc.)
   - Steps to reproduce
   - Potential impact
   - A proof-of-concept (if applicable)

### Response Timeline
- We will acknowledge receipt of your vulnerability report within 48 hours.
- We will provide a status update on our investigation within 5 business days.
- If the vulnerability is confirmed, we will work with you on a timeline for releasing a patch.

## Dependency Updates
We regularly monitor dependencies for known vulnerabilities and update them as part of our normal release cycle. Critical security updates are expedited.
